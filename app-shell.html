Celeste App Shell connecting

1 · App source

Paste a complete HTML app. If it already carries a Celeste bridge it is left untouched and this shell answers as its host; if it has none, the standard bridge is injected. Either way its tool calls are proxied through this shell's live connection.

2 · Tool console

Call a tool directly, with no app involved — the quickest way to confirm the connectors are reachable.

Every proxied and direct call, newest first. Results are summarised, never dumped.
